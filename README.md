## 📌 Essential Sites List

We built this list to guide ourselves and help the community grow.  
*(Click on the names to visit the sites)*

### 🌐 Platforms to create sites
Where the magic happens. Create and manage your sites directly on these platforms.
* **[Wapkiz](https://wapkiz.org)** — The main and most popular platform for creating dynamic and free mobile sites.
* **[Wapaxo](https://wapaxo.com)** — An excellent emerging alternative for site creators with similar and innovative features.

### 📤 Project Sharing
Download codes and share your creations with other developers.
* **[ThemeHub](https://themehub.qzz.io)** — The central hub for downloading and sharing projects and scripts developed for the Wapkiz platform.

### 🎨 Themes and Templates
Give your site a professional look using ready-made themes.
* **[FfThemes](https://ffthemes.wapkizs.com)** — A great library with various free themes to apply to your site.
* **[WapkizThemes](https://templatesfor.wapkizs.com)** — Exclusively focused on modern and responsive templates for the Wapkiz ecosystem.

### 💬 Forums
Connect with the community, ask questions, and network.
* **[WapForum](https://wapforum.wapkizs.com)** — The main meeting point for the community for discussions, support, and sharing ideas.
* *More forums coming soon...*

### 📚 Tutorials and Tips
Learn new techniques, codes (HTML/CSS/Wapkiz tags), and optimizations.
* **[WapkizTips](https://tipsandtricksfor.wapkizs.com)** — Detailed tutorials, tips, and hidden tricks to get the most out of your site.

## 🤝 How to contribute

Do you want to help make this list even more complete? All contributions are welcome! Follow these steps:

1. **Fork** this repository.
2. Create a branch for your modification (`git checkout -b my-contribution`).
3. Add the new site in the correct category (or create a new one if needed), keeping the format: `* **[Name] URL** - Description`.
4. Commit your changes (`git commit -m 'Adding new tutorial site'`).
5. Push to the branch (`git push origin my-contribution`).
6. Open a **Pull Request** briefly explaining your suggestion.

> ✅ **Criteria for inclusion:** Sites must be free (or have a good free plan), relevant to Wapkiz/Wapaxo site building, and strictly free of malicious content or piracy.
